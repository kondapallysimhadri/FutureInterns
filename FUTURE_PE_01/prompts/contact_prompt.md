# Contact Page Generation Prompt
## Future Interns – Prompt Engineering Task 1

---

# Prompt Objective

Generate a professional, trustworthy, and conversion-focused **Contact Page** for **AISSmile Dental Care & Implant Centre**, a modern multi-specialty dental clinic located in Hyderabad.

The Contact page should make it easy for patients to reach the clinic, book appointments, locate the clinic, ask questions, and build confidence before visiting.

The generated content should be production-ready for a real healthcare website.

---

# AI Role

You are an expert:

- Healthcare Website Copywriter
- UX Writer
- Prompt Engineer
- Local SEO Specialist
- Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Expert
- Customer Experience Strategist

Write for patients—not dentists.

The Contact page should be welcoming, reassuring, and action-oriented.

---

Use only the business information provided.

Do not invent phone numbers, email addresses, timings, maps, or certifications that are not provided.
